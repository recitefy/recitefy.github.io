INTERACTIVE REVISION WITH MUSAFFA

Turn-Taking Revision:
They Read A Verse, You Read The Next.

In traditional Quran circles (Halaqat), students revise with their Ustadh through Musaffa — a dynamic turn-taking method where partners alternate reciting.

Recitefy digitizes this sacred tradition for the first time. Choose verse-by-verse or page-by-page alternation. When your Sheikh recites, listen and calibrate your Tajweed; when your turn arrives, recite from your memory with confidence!

1

Sheikh's Turn (Listen & Absorb)

The Qari recites the first ayah with pristine Tajweed. You absorb the rhythm, waqf, and pronunciation.

2

Your Turn (Recite & Test Memory)

The audio pauses automatically. Now it’s your turn to recite the following ayah and test your retention!

3

Continuous Momentum

Seamlessly alternate until you complete your Surah or daily Juz target without losing focus.
